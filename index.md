# Last Orders

Last Orders is a diary of the pubs that you visit. Your diary stays on your phone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking.

Last Orders is for adults only.

- [Privacy policy](privacy)
- [Terms of use](terms)

## Contact

Email privacy@lastorders.email.

## The pub list

The app finds pubs in a list on your phone. The list comes from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). © OpenStreetMap contributors. The data is available under the [Open Database Licence](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).

The app can download a new list from [`/pubs/manifest.json`](pubs/manifest.json).
