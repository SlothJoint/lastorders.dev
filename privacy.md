# Last Orders privacy policy

Last changed: 22 November 2026

Last Orders is a diary of the pubs that you visit. Your diary stays on your phone. You have no account, and we can't see your check-ins, drinks or photos. This policy tells you what the app keeps, what leaves your phone, and what your rights are.

## Who we are

George Skinner makes Last Orders.

- Email: privacy@lastorders.email

## What we collect

Nothing. We don't run a server for the app, and we receive no data about you or your diary. We don't use analytics, advertising or crash reports.

## What the app keeps on your phone

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your check-ins: the pub, the time that you arrived and left | This is your diary. |
| The route that you walked between pubs, if you turn on "Record my route between pubs" | To show the streets that you walked on your crawl card, and the distance. |
| Your drinks, food, captions and photos | So that you can see your visits. |
| The names and card stats of your nights | To show your crawl cards the way that you chose. |
| Your settings: theme, crawl cards, automatic check-in | To give you the app the way that you chose. |
| Your answer to "Are you 18 or over?" | So that the app asks only once. |
| The UK pub list | To find pubs near you, without the internet. |

When you delete the app, your phone deletes all of this. You can also delete one check-in, one night or all your check-ins in More > Your data.

## Your location

- **Pubs near you and checking in.** The app uses your phone's location only on your phone. It finds pubs in the pub list, and makes sure that you are within 50 m of the pub. The app doesn't keep your location, and it doesn't send it anywhere.
- **Your route between pubs.** "Record my route between pubs" is off until you turn it on. When it is on, the app records your location after each check-in, and your phone shows a notification while it records. The app keeps only your walks between two pubs of one night. It deletes the rest, such as your walk home. The route stays on your phone. A picture that you share shows the route only if you turn on "Show the route that I walked".
- **Automatic check-in.** "Suggest check-ins" and "Leave pubs automatically" are off until you turn them on. When one is on, your phone watches circles round the pubs near you, also when the app isn't open. Your phone needs to allow location "all the time" for this. Your location stays on your phone.

## Photos

Before the app keeps a photo, it makes a new copy of it. The copy has no hidden data, such as the GPS position where you took the photo. The app keeps photos in its own folder on your phone.

## What leaves your phone

| What | Where it goes | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your phone's internet address and the area of the map that you look at | OpenFreeMap, which sends the map pictures | When you look at a map |
| Your phone's internet address | GitHub, which runs lastorders.dev | Once a week at most, on Wi-Fi, when the app asks for a newer pub list |
| A picture of a crawl | The app that you choose in your phone's share menu | Only when you share a crawl |
| Your diary, without photos | Your own Google account (Android backup) | When backup is on in your phone's settings |
| A file with your diary | The place that you choose in your phone's share menu | Only when you tap "Export my diary" |

We don't receive any of these. After you share a picture or a file, the privacy policy of the app or service that you chose applies to it.

## Notifications

When "Suggest check-ins" is on, your phone shows "Are you at [pub]?" when you stay at a pub. Your phone makes this notification itself. It doesn't come from a server.

## Our lawful basis

We don't process your personal data, because it stays on your phone. If you email us, we use your email to reply to you (legitimate interests), and we delete it when we no longer need it.

## Your rights

Your data is on your phone, so you control it: you can see it, change it, export it and delete it in the app. If you have a question about your data or this policy, email privacy@lastorders.email.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

## Age

Last Orders is only for people who are 18 or over.

## Changes to this policy

If we make an important change, we tell you in the app before the change starts. The date at the top shows when we last changed this policy.
